# CRYCHIC v0.1 Exploratory Baseline Method Specification

Status: **normative for the implemented v0.1 exploratory baseline**

Method version: `0.1.0-exploratory`

Last reviewed: 2026-07-12

This document restores the equations needed by the first runnable CRYCHIC
baseline. It does not authorize inferential p-values, q-values, calibrated
active probabilities, or causal sender claims. Those fields remain missing
until the v0.3 calibration gates in `DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md` are passed.

## Analysis Units

Cells contribute to aggregation only. The biological resampling unit is
`subject_id`; `sample_id` identifies a library or sampled context. A sample
must map to exactly one subject and one canonical context tuple.

For sample `u`, cell type `c`, and gene `j`, raw counts are aggregated as

$$
Y_{ucj} = \sum_{i \in (u,c)} Y_{ij}.
$$

The aggregate also retains the number of cells `n_uc`, the sample-wide cell
proportion `pi_uc`, raw detection fraction, and a state-eligibility flag.
When no eligible cell exists, state expression is missing rather than zero.
Normalized-only inputs use the arithmetic mean on their declared scale and
are represented by a separate exploratory aggregate contract.
`allow_duplicate_genes=True` is limited to validation and descriptive input
inspection; baseline fitting and persisted result keys require unique gene IDs.

## Expression And Detection Availability

For counts, library-normalized expression is

$$
x_{ucj} = 10^6 \frac{Y_{ucj}}{\sum_h Y_{uch}}.
$$

Detection is shrunk with an explicit beta-binomial posterior mean,

$$
\widetilde d_{ucj} =
\frac{k_{ucj} + a}{n_{uc} + a + b},
$$

where `k_ucj` is the number of cells with a nonzero count. The implemented
default `a=b=0.5` is Jeffreys smoothing, not an inferred biological prior.
Gene availability is mapped to `[0, 1]` using

$$
a_{ucj} = \frac{x_{ucj}}{x_{ucj}+\kappa_j}
\widetilde d_{ucj}^{\rho},
$$

The implemented v0.1 default uses fixed `kappa=1`, Hill coefficient one, and
`rho=1`. These parameters are frozen for the full contrast and may be
overridden explicitly; v0.1 does not learn context-specific transforms.
For normalized-only input without declared nondetection semantics, the
detection factor is fixed to one. It is not Beta-shrunk with `n_uc`, so cell
count cannot create an artificial state-availability difference.

For a required complex `C` with `m` subunits, availability is the generalized
harmonic soft minimum

$$
A_{ucC} =
\left[
\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j \in C}(a_{ucj}+\epsilon)^{-p}
\right]^{-1/p} - \epsilon,
$$

clipped to `[0, 1]`. All required subunits must be mapped. A missing required
subunit makes the complex unavailable with an auditable reason; it is not
silently discarded.

## State And Ecosystem Availability

For sender `s`, receiver `r`, and ligand-receptor interaction `k`, the state
availability is

$$
M^{state}_{usrk} = A_{usL_k} A_{urR_k}.
$$

It answers whether eligible sender and receiver cell states express the
required molecular components. The capture-weighted ecosystem proxy is

$$
M^{ecosystem}_{usrk} = M^{state}_{usrk}
\sqrt{\pi_{us}\pi_{ur}}.
$$

This is not absolute tissue communication. Structural absence propagates as
missing state evidence and is represented separately from a measured zero.

## Continuous Receiver Response

The baseline response uses sample-level log-CPM values, balanced across
biological samples rather than cells. For receiver `r`, gene `j`, and context
`g`, let `m_grj` be the equally weighted mean of eligible sample aggregates.
The global contrast is

$$
\Delta^{global}_{grj} = m_{grj} -
\sum_{h \ne g}\omega_h m_{hrj},
\qquad \sum_h \omega_h=1.
$$

The topology-local contrast is

$$
\Delta^{local}_{grj} = m_{grj} -
\sum_{h \in N(g)}\omega_{gh}m_{hrj}.
$$

For paired exploratory summaries, differences are first formed within
subject and then averaged. If receiver eligibility leaves a partially paired
two-context table, only complete subject pairs are used after the declared
minimum pair-support check; the method is explicitly labeled complete-case and
single-context samples do not enter that contrast. Standard errors and
standardized effects are diagnostics only in v0.1; no reference-distribution
p-value is emitted.

## Ligand-Target Attribution

Let `W_jk >= 0` be a versioned NicheNet ligand-target prior and `Z_grj` the
positive, direction-compatible response channel. The gated basis is

$$
B_{grjk} = W_{jk} G^{receptor}_{grk},
$$

with each eligible column normalized to unit L2 norm. The v0.1 experimental
baseline fits each receiver/global-contrast branch using non-negative elastic
net,

$$
\widehat\alpha_{gr} = \arg\min_{\alpha \ge 0}
\frac{1}{2}\lVert Q_{gr}^{1/2}(Z_{gr}-B_{gr}\alpha)\rVert_2^2
+ \lambda_1\lVert\alpha\rVert_1
+ \frac{\lambda_2}{2}\lVert\alpha\rVert_2^2.
$$

Only ligands linked to an eligible LR interaction and receiver receptor are
considered. Receptor gates are pooled without consulting context labels.
Negative receiver responses are retained in the signed residual; they are not
claimed to be explained by a non-negative prior. Joint
graph-fused fitting, equivalence-class stability, and cross-fitted tuning are
planned for v0.2 and are not part of this baseline.

The explained and residual responses are

$$
\widehat Z_{gr}=B_{gr}\widehat\alpha_{gr}, \qquad
R_{gr}=Z^{signed}_{gr}-\widehat Z_{gr}.
$$

### Versioned downstream attribution support

The default v0.1 downstream scaling remains
`gated_prior_attribution_v1`,

$$
s^{(1)}_j =
\frac{\widehat\alpha_j}{\max_k \widehat\alpha_k},
$$

with zero support when the denominator is zero. This historical default is
unchanged. A post-benchmark, explicitly opt-in candidate is available as
`gated_response_norm_attribution_support_v2`,

$$
s^{(2)}_j = \operatorname{clip}_{[0,1]}
\left(\frac{\lVert B_j\widehat\alpha_j\rVert_2}
{\lVert Z^+\rVert_2}\right).
$$

Because `B_j` is the receptor-gated basis column, v2 retains the frozen gate
in its numerator and scales weak fitted contributions by the magnitude of the
complete positive response channel. The selected support multiplies the same
sample target-activity projection used by v1. It can only be enabled with the
workflow key `downstream_attribution_support_method`; unknown values are
rejected. V2 writes its formula, numerator, denominator, clipping interval,
gate-aware status and application rule to the result manifest. Selecting v1
explicitly is numerically and provenance-equivalent to omitting the option.
Neither support version is inferential.

## Non-Causal Sender Evidence

For each context, receiver, and interaction, ligand availability is first
averaged within subject. A candidate sender retains three descriptive
components: mean ligand availability, its share among candidate cell types,
and subject prevalence above a frozen threshold. Their fixed weighted mean is
converted to an assignment weight by a common-temperature softmax. Candidate
weights sum to one within a context/receiver/interaction group.

This assignment consumes no receiver outcome or attribution coefficient.
Adjusted sender-receiver coupling is disabled in v0.1, so the result is
expression evidence and never a causal source claim.

## Exploratory Integrated Strength

When attribution is available, components are retained separately and the
descriptive strength is their weighted geometric mean,

$$
S_{usrk} =
\left[
(M_{usrk})^{w_M}
(D_{grk})^{w_D}
(C_{grsk})^{w_C}
(Q_k)^{w_Q}
\right]^{1/(w_M+w_D+w_C+w_Q)},
$$

Availability `M`, downstream impact `D`, sender evidence `C`, and resource
quality `Q` all lie in `[0,1]`. One frozen scoring functional is reused across
all contexts in a contrast. The v0.1 functional is in-sample and is explicitly
tagged `exploratory_not_cross_fitted`.
If no compatible target prior is available, the output is explicitly named
`availability_baseline`; it is never called a probability or full CRYCHIC
strength.

## Output Semantics

| Quantity | v0.1 status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| availability state/ecosystem | implemented | expression/detection baseline |
| receiver effect and diagnostic SE | implemented | exploratory sample-level contrast |
| non-negative ligand attribution | experimental | direction-compatible target explanation |
| `comm_strength` | experimental | component-preserving descriptive score |
| `comm_probability` | unavailable | reserved for calibrated active-null posterior |
| p-value/q-value | unavailable | reserved for full-pipeline resampling gates |
| sender causal source | never claimed | soft expression evidence is not causality |

All unavailable inferential fields are persisted as null with a reason code.
